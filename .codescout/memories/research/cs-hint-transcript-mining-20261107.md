# Mining real transcripts for [cs-verified] and [cs-hint] deliveries (2026-11-07)

Follow-up to [[cs-verified-product-path-20261106]]. Descriptive only: no control arm exists, because the hooks are always on. Scripts were `mine1.py` to `mine6.py` in the session scratchpad (read-only, not kept; they reuse `parse` from `tools/hint-channel-eval/score.py`).

## Scope
- 19 transcripts (14 main sessions plus 5 subagent files under `<sid>/subagents/`) of the codescout project in the four profiles, modified after `CLAUDE.local.md` (2026-11-05 18:49:58Z). Sessions started before that time did not load the declaration (compaction reload not checked).
- Only genuine hook deliveries count: `attachment` entries of type `hook_additional_context`. Other `[cs-hint]` text in transcripts is quoted docs or discussion (38 tool results and 9 user strings in this scan). A string count is wrong.

## [cs-verified]
- 4 deliveries since the change, all on probe files the sessions made to test the hook (`addOne`, `addNumbers`, `a`). Zero in real work.
- 5 results kept `advice` with `verified:false` (3 probes, 2 real edits). In the 2 real edits the agent looked up no callers.
- Nothing here says anything about the declaration's effect.

## [cs-hint] (buddy plugin, `buddy/scripts/cs_heuristics.py`; 64 deliveries, 3 of them self-generated by the mining session and excluded)
Fire rules, read from the source:
- structural edit: `edit_file` on a code extension, new_string at least 2 lines, a line starting `def `, `fn `, `class `, `struct `, `impl `, `enum `, `trait `, `interface `, `func `, `function `.
- grep concept: pattern at least 12 chars, 2+ all-alpha words, no regex metacharacters, no code tokens. It cannot tell a concept from a literal phrase.
- parallel write: two write-class calls in the same second.

Results:
- edit_code nudge (20 external): 13 came after the server had already refused the edit (IL-2 gate), 11 of them then used `edit_code`; the hint is redundant there and cannot be credited. 7 came after `edit_file` succeeded: 1 switched to `edit_code`. Of 6 read, 2 added new functions (95 and 104 lines) and 4 modified existing definitions; the hint applied by its own rule in all 6.
- grep literal (17): by my manual label none was a concept query (error messages, doc phrases, `class X` fragments). 1 of 17 switched tool. Ignoring was correct. Labels are my reading of the pattern and the next calls only.
- parallel writes (18): all 18 had 2+ writes in one batch. Repeat within 10 calls: 2 of 14 before the declaration, 0 of 4 after. No control.
- uncommitted changes (6): not scored.

## Inventory of additionalContext sources (scripts `mine7.py` to `mine9.py`)
Scope: 21 transcript files, 203,591,767 bytes. Counted: `hook_additional_context` attachments, 177 blocks, 421,036 bytes.
- SessionStart superpowers intro: 43 blocks, 146,931 B. SessionStart MEMORIES list: 38 blocks, 193,151 B. SessionStart PROJECT BOOTSTRAP: 9 blocks, 46,649 B. Together 387 KB, about 92% of the bytes.
- All `[cs-hint]`: 64 blocks, about 14.6 KB (about 3.5%). `[cs-verified]`: 4 blocks, about 1 KB. The remaining 19 blocks are recon-before-edit, the first-Agent-dispatch hint and persisted large outputs.
- `hook_success` records show 108 `SessionStart:compact` runs against 14 `startup` and 2 `resume`. The SessionStart cost therefore grows with the number of compactions, not sessions.
- Largest `[cs-hint]` per delivery: uncommitted changes, about 1.2 KB (6 deliveries, not scored). The other hints are 100 to 200 B.
- About 7,000 `PreToolUse` runs on codescout tools emit only `permissionDecision: allow` (141 B). That is a permission decision, not model context. Read as samples for `run_command`, `grep` and `edit_file`; not every run was read.
- Limits: transcript bytes are not context bytes. The harness records such as `prompt_snapshot` (8.9 MB), `output_style` (5.3 MB) and `invoked_skills` (5.3 MB) were not checked for visibility to the model.

## Limits
- Small samples, one project. Windows of 3, 5 and 10 calls are my choice and were not tested for sensitivity.
- The declaration covers only messages marked `[cs-verified]`, so the pre/post split is not a test of the declaration.
- The transcript set grows while it is measured: the mining session's own edits added 3 deliveries between two runs (found by the count change and one direct sample).
- Wrong claim made and corrected in the session: that the structural-edit hint fires on any `edit_file`. It fires by the rule above.
