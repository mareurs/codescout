## Approaches

1. **Chosen: transfer and ablate executable existing-check feedback.** Broaden a source-qualified compile check, offer its exact plan to low, and separately enforce it at completion. This is a transfer test of an existing mechanism, not a novelty claim. Full common Codescout/onboarding/CLAUDE/Recon remains in every condition; no intentionally crippled file-tool baseline.
