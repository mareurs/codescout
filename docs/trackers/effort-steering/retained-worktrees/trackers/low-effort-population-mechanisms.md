# Low effort quality mechanisms — population-bound acceptance

## What we measure

A continuation of low-effort-quality-mechanisms-snapshot-workflow.md (catalog35c4c336bad905fb, born in worktree9369), which is preserved unchanged. The target is low effort delivering task-preserving accepted work at lower whole model-client cost than xhigh. A checker can improve feedback/acceptance authority; it cannot itself establish model quality. Actual billing and orchestration/CPU cost are not measured by model-client estimates.

Population-transfer-20261101 prospectively fixes two tasks, three arms each, all six configured slots, independent full source-bound test enumeration/execution, and source review. Required cases cannot disappear; newly added cases are allowed and must pass. Candidate cost/quality and acceptance-gain mechanism contrasts are separate; all-pass is a ceiling. Public tasks and repeated repo families prevent a training/repo holdout claim.
