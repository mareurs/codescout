## End goal and completion criterion

Find a reproducible automatic workflow that preserves the independently evaluated quality of an accepted xhigh workflow while reducing its whole-workflow model-client cost. Passing the currently known tests alone is insufficient.

Completion requires a frozen procedure, prospective new cases, common independent behavioral/compatibility evaluation, a matched xhigh workflow with equivalent checking/repair access, and lower complete cost including discovery, failed attempts, repair and escalation. Fix the sample, quality margin, cost and stopping rules before payment. The latest two-task diagnostic did fix these, but stopped invalid and low's tested terminal artifact failed. A broader external cohort and demonstrated mechanism are still outstanding; the end goal has not been met.

Separate four questions: can the checker detect the defect, did the actor actually receive the feedback, did it correct the source, and does the complete workflow transfer? A pass at one stage does not establish the next.
