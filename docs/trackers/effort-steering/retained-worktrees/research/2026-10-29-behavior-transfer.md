Does one complete failing behavioral-check log at Stop extend the observed compiler-feedback repair mechanism to another task? This is a new historical runtime-config task in the already represented bounded-filesystem area of Codescout. It is not a new independent task family or external-repository validation.

## Interpretation

The two low candidates matched xhigh on this finite independent contract at lower measured model cost. This does not show the feedback gate caused an improvement: no candidate exposed a failing developer check and no repair was requested. There is no new behavioral feedback→edit→pass chain. Prompt precision, available shared infrastructure and context completeness were not ablated.

This repeats the inactive-gate limitation already recorded in LEM-7, LEM-8 and LEM-9, rather than adding another causal repair success to LEM-13. The task is useful as an acceptance/control observation but does not discriminate the correction mechanism. Do not spend another batch repeating this same-area task to establish that effect.

The next informative design needs a separate calibration pool with naturally observed behavioral failures, a frozen validator/selection rule, then disjoint external-repository holdout tasks. Calibration failures must not be promoted into holdout evidence. No such external validation was performed here. X added tests and attempted broader checks; matching finite acceptance does not establish equal overall verification quality.
